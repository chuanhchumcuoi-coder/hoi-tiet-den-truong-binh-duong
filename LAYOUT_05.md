# Layout 05 - Tinh gon khu thoi tiet

- An dong mo ta duoi tieu de thoi tiet.
- An thanh trang thai cap nhat nam giua tieu de va hai the thoi tiet.
- An dong giai thich nguon dai o cuoi khu thoi tiet.
- Dua `Nguon: <ten nguon>` vao goc phai chan cua tung the thoi tiet.
- Giu nguyen thoi gian du lieu o goc trai chan the.
- Cac logic thoi tiet, GPS, tuyen duong, canh bao va loi khuyen khong thay doi.
